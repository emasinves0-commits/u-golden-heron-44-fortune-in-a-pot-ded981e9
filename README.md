A one-line fortune for a houseplant on a windowsill, written for the "Fortune in a Pot" bounty on make.fun. The plant promises a leaf so dramatic that its human will fuss over it more than over the household cat. `main.ts` validates the line (1 to 120 characters, not blank) and writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.

> Soon, your next leaf will unfurl with such drama the human will pet it more often than they pet the actual cat.
